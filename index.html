Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about HexaPrep.

Is HexaPrep really free?

Yes, HexaPrep is 100% free. There are no paywalls, no hidden fees, and no account requirements to access our study guides and practice mock tests.

Our practice exams are highly realistic. We focus heavily on scenario-based questions that mimic the actual difficulty, distractors, and structure of the real FINRA SIE and Series 7 exams.
